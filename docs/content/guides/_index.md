Start with [sessions and tiers](/guides/authentication/).
It is the one that shapes every other read: what tier 0 gives you, what the two cookies add, and what Facebook does when it decides you have asked enough.

- [Sessions and tiers](/guides/authentication/): what reads without anything, what needs two cookies, and how Facebook says no.
- [Pages and profiles](/guides/pages-and-profiles/): one read for the whole shell, the About tab, and the timeline's one post.
- [Posts and comments](/guides/posts-and-comments/): a permalink whole, with entities, comments and all seven reaction types.
- [Media](/guides/media/): photos with alt text and album neighbours, videos with captions and transcripts, and the bytes.
- [Groups and events](/guides/groups-and-events/): the group shell and its discussion, and events from the tab or a permalink.
- [Finding your way in](/guides/search-and-discovery/): classify anything with `fb id`, ask `fb explain` what fb would do, and find seeds.
- [The claim graph](/guides/discovering/): what a read said about everything else, walked with `fb graph` and written as RDF.
- [Building a store](/guides/datasets/): `fb crawl` into SQLite, `fb query` in plain SQL, `fb export` as RDF.
- [Capturing a read](/guides/archiving/): the HTML, the Relay payloads and the headers, which is how a bug gets reported.
- [HTTP and MCP](/guides/serving/): the same eighteen operations over a port or over stdio.
